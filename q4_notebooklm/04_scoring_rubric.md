# Scoring rubric (0 to 2 per criterion, 8 points per feature)

| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Accuracy** | One or more factual errors or invented facts | Minor imprecision or omission, no invented facts | All checked claims match the answer key |
| **Relevance** | Off-task or generic | Mostly on-task, some padding | Directly answers the academic task |
| **Citation faithfulness** (check 3 citations) | Citation missing or does not support the claim | Supports the claim partially | All three citations support the claim |
| **Usefulness in academic workflow** | Would not use | Useful after substantial editing | Usable with light checking; saves real time |

Also record, per feature: **time taken**, **limitations observed** (bias, hallucination, shallow coverage, over-confident tone, missing nuance) and **over-reliance risk** (could a student submit this without understanding it?).

Hallucination probes are scored Pass / Partial / Fail.

# Demonstration video: script and shot list (target 1:50, must be under 2:00)

Audience: senior executives. Tone: business value first, then a short live demo, then the design rationale.
Record with the Teams / PowerPoint / OBS screen recorder, 1080p, microphone on. Upload to Microsoft Stream (OneDrive) and share with the teaching team ("People in La Trobe with the link" or add the lecturer by name).

| Time | Screen | Voice-over (read naturally) |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:15 | Title slide: "GreenDesk: ESG incident reporting assistant" | "Every week our sites waste water and energy through leaks, lights and air-con left on, and contaminated recycling. Most of it is never reported, because reporting means finding the right form or the right person. GreenDesk fixes that." |
| 0:15-0:30 | Conversation map diagram | "GreenDesk is a Dialogflow CX chatbot with four flows: incident reporting, recycling guidance, escalation to a person, and a welcome menu. It captures structured data that goes straight to the right team and into our ESG dashboard." |
| 0:30-1:05 | Simulator or Messenger: T1 happy path. Type: "There's a water leak in Building C that has been running all morning" | "Watch: from one sentence it already knows the issue, the building and the urgency, so it only asks for the exact location. It confirms everything, offers anonymous reporting, and routes the report to Facilities and Energy as Priority 2, same business day." |
| 1:05-1:25 | T2: "there's a chemical spill in the warehouse and it's spreading" | "Safety comes first. Any hazardous spill, or anything urgent, triggers an immediate safety message with the emergency number and Priority 1 escalation to the on-call team." |
| 1:25-1:40 | Show "talk to a person" or a no-match, then the parameter panel | "If it can't understand someone after three tries, or they ask for a person, it hands over to the ESG Operations Desk. Personal details are redacted from logs, and confidential conduct issues are redirected to our Speak Up line." |
| 1:40-1:50 | Cloud workflow diagram | "Next step: a three-month pilot at two sites, connected to our ticketing system and ESG dashboard. We'll measure reporting volume, routing accuracy and time to fix. Thank you." |

Checklist: under 2:00; face camera optional; captions on (Stream auto-captions); test that the link opens in a private browser window while signed in as a La Trobe user.

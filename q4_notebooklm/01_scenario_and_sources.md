# Scenario and source pack

## Scenario: "ESG governance workshop briefing"

A Master of Business Analytics student is preparing for a seminar on **Australia's mandatory climate-related financial disclosure regime** and must (1) write a two-page briefing for a governance workshop, (2) revise the topic for an assessment, and (3) compare how two listed companies report climate risk. Their lecturer wants to turn the same material into revision resources for the class, and a researcher wants to scope related literature.

This mirrors ESG work that graduates do in practice: reading a standard and regulator guidance, then turning them into a briefing and a comparison.

## Notebook name

`BUS5001 A3 - AU climate disclosure briefing`
